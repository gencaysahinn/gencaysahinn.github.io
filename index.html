About the Game

Uçan Böcek: Istanbul is a casual one-touch arcade game designed for mobile devices. Take control of a fearless, leather-jacket-wearing cockroach flying over a stylized pixel-art Istanbul skyline—featuring the Bosphorus Bridge, Maiden's Tower, and gritty industrial pipe mazes. Compete for high scores via Google Play Games Services and unlock iconic character styles.

🏙️ Stylized Istanbul Skyline

Fly past pixel-art landmarks including the Bosphorus Bridge and Maiden's Tower under dynamic drifting clouds.

🧥 Iconic Roach Characters

Play as the classic Leather-Jacket Uçan Böcek and unlock fun themed characters inspired by viral internet culture.

🏆 Google Play Games

Sign in with Google Play Games Services to track your best runs on global leaderboards and unlock achievements.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Uçan Böcek: Istanbul ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects user information when you play our mobile game on Android.

1. Information Collected via Third-Party Services

The App uses trusted third-party SDKs to provide core gameplay features and advertising. These services may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Games Services: Used for player authentication, cloud score saving, achievements, and global leaderboards. We only access your public gamer profile ID and display name required to display leaderboard rankings.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display in-app advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising IDs, device information, and coarse location data to serve relevant or non-personalized ads.

2. How We Use Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data processed through Google Play Games Services and AdMob is strictly used to:

  • Authenticate players and maintain leaderboard standings.
  • Save game progress and unlocked character cosmetics.
  • Deliver and measure in-app advertisements to keep the game free to play.

3. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can revoke Google Play Games Services permissions or delete your Play Games profile data at any time through your Google Account settings or the Google Play Games app. If you have questions or wish to request assistance regarding your data, please contact us using the support details below.

4. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
